# Security review, Batch 6.2

A read-only review of the whole app on 27 Oct 2026 (code at `c79a361`), looking for attacks that work, not general advice. It traced every Server Action, route handler, raw SQL query, upload and message sender. This page lists what it found and what was done. Run it again before launch on whatever has changed since.

## Findings

| #   | Severity | Finding                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Status                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| --- | -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| H1  | High     | Any signed-in Customer could ask for a Stock Alert on an in-stock Product. The next cron tick sent it and deleted it, and the script asked again: thousands of jobs a minute, ahead of Order confirmations, Substitution texts and refunds, each costing an SMS | Fixed: alerts only for sold-out Products, at most 20 open per Customer (`inventory/internal/alerts.ts`)                                                                                                                                                |
| M1  | Medium   | One account could hold the whole Hub's stock with unpaid online Orders, placing a new one as each Payment Window ended                                                                                                                                          | Fixed: one unpaid online Order at a time, and at most 200 items an Order (`placeOrder`); 28 Oct: Admins can block an account from ordering on the Order page                                                                                           |
| M2  | Medium   | Sign-in codes could be texted to any number 10 times a quarter hour from each IP, all day: an SMS bomb and a cost sink                                                                                                                                          | Fixed: 10 codes a day per number, and at most 200 codes an hour in all (then the email link only, and an error in the log). Gmail dots and `+tags` count as one inbox. Still open: a CAPTCHA (Cloudflare Turnstile) once the Cloudflare account exists |
| L1  | Low      | Delivery PIN tries were read, compared and counted in separate steps, so tries sent at once could check far more than 5 PINs                                                                                                                                    | Fixed: one guarded `UPDATE` checks the PIN and counts a wrong one under the row's lock                                                                                                                                                                 |
| L2  | Low      | No security headers                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Fixed: `frame-ancestors 'none'`, `X-Frame-Options`, `nosniff`, `Referrer-Policy` (`next.config.ts`). A full Content Security Policy waits until third-party scripts (payments, maps, Sentry) are known                                                 |
| L3  | Low      | The offline copy of Home and Product pages (service worker) kept "Buy again" and Shopping List names after sign-out                                                                                                                                             | Fixed: signing out deletes the stored pages                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| L4  | Low      | A small photo file that unpacks into gigabytes of pixels could crash a claim or photo upload                                                                                                                                                                    | Fixed: photos over 50 megapixels are refused before decoding, and decoding is capped too                                                                                                                                                               |
| L5  | Low      | Voucher codes could be guessed without limit                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Fixed: 10 tries a quarter hour per IP and per account                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| L6  | Low      | Order texts go to the contact number typed at Checkout, unverified                                                                                                                                                                                              | Accepted: bounded by 10 Orders an hour; Customers need to send to a family member's number                                                                                                                                                             |
| L7  | Low      | A Vercel deployment with `NODE_ENV=development` and `E2E_MAILBOX=1` would open `/dev/sign-in` and the dev mailbox                                                                                                                                               | Fixed: test mode is always off on Vercel; `NODE_ENV` removed from `.env.example`                                                                                                                                                                       |
| L8  | Low      | A Rider can record less cash than due with a note                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Accepted: the cash page already shows each Order where the cash differs from what was due, with the note                                                                                                                                               |
| L9  | Low      | A referred friend's first name went into our SMS and email unchanged, so it could carry a link                                                                                                                                                                  | Fixed: only letters, 20 at most                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| L10 | Low      | Server Actions accept bodies up to 26 MB (for photos) before any check                                                                                                                                                                                          | Accepted for now; move uploads to signed-in route handlers if abuse appears                                                                                                                                                                            |
| L11 | Info     | The seed makes Customers with real-format mobile numbers and could be run against production                                                                                                                                                                    | Fixed: the seed refuses remote databases unless `ALLOW_REMOTE_SEED=1`                                                                                                                                                                                  |

Also found while writing the runbook, and fixed:

- An email that failed stopped the text for the same notice (Order confirmation, Substitution, Delivery PIN, refund, Stock Alert): every channel is now tried, and the job retries only what failed.
- "Delivered without the PIN" could not save a cash amount different from what was due: the form now has the "why" box.

## Checked and sound

Every one of the 24 `"use server"` files checks the signed-in role itself; Orders, claims, addresses, lists and alerts are scoped to their owner; totals, refunds and claim paybacks are recomputed and capped on the server; webhooks are verified in constant time and applied once; no raw SQL takes unparameterised input; uploads are re-encoded; the dev pages and the stand-in payment provider are off in production; cookies are HTTP-only and `Secure` in production; no secret is in a `NEXT_PUBLIC_*` variable.

## Hardening for later

Done 29 Oct: stop links carry their purpose and end after 90 days (`lib/signed.ts`). Done 28 Oct: Order notices run ahead of reminders and alerts in the jobs queue; the cron deletes ended sign-in sessions and codes.

- Claim photos private (signed URLs) when the R2 media store lands; today they are public files with random names.
- `clientIp` trusts the first `x-forwarded-for` hop, which is right on Vercel; revisit if a proxy is put in front.
- Dependencies: `npm audit --omit=dev` finds nothing. `npm audit fix` (27 Oct) moved the shadcn CLI to 4.23, which dropped the `fast-uri` and `body-parser` advisories; what is left is 3 low advisories in `deepmerge`, inside the shadcn CLI (a dev tool), with no non-breaking fix.
